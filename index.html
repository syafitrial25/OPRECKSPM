01 / ABOUT US

A Space to Learn. A Community to Grow.

Kelompok Studi Pasar Modal FEB UPN "Veteran" Jakarta is a student organization dedicated to expanding capital market knowledge, encouraging collaboration, and developing students' potential through meaningful experiences.

KSPM.
