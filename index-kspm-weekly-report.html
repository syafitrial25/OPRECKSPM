Get to Know Us

About Our Community.

Kelompok Studi Pasar Modal (KSPM) FEB UPN "Veteran" Jakarta is a student organization that focuses on capital market education and investment knowledge.

KSPM provides a space for students to learn, explore, and develop their understanding of the capital market through educational activities, discussions, and collaborative experiences.

Learn. Connect. Grow.

Building knowledge, creating connections, and growing together as a capital market community.
