WHO WE ARE

About Our Community.

KSPM FEB UPNVJ is a student community that focuses on capital market education and financial literacy. We provide a space for students to learn, explore, and develop their understanding of the investment world.

Through education, collaboration, and various activities, we aim to create a generation that is knowledgeable, responsible, and ready to face the future of finance.
